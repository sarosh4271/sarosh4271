## About me

I am a product-focused Senior Mobile Engineer with **5+ years of experience** building, scaling, and releasing cross-platform applications. My work spans connected hardware, sports technology, event platforms, student communities, and location-aware consumer products.

I enjoy solving the difficult parts of mobile engineering: reliable BLE communication, native platform interoperability, maintainable architecture, polished interfaces, automated delivery, and turning complex product requirements into dependable systems.

## What I work with

- **Mobile:** Flutter, Dart, Riverpod, Clean Architecture, MVVM, responsive UI
- **BLE, IoT & native:** BLE/GATT, device protocols, Kotlin, Swift, Android SDKs, iOS SDKs, platform channels
- **Services:** REST APIs, Firebase, Node.js, Google Maps, push notifications, payments, subscriptions
- **Delivery & product:** GitHub Actions, Fastlane, CI/CD, Mixpanel, Adapty, Superwall, App Store, Google Play

## Career highlights

- Shipped **15+ projects**, including **10+ BLE applications** for connected hardware and IoT devices.
- Released **5 production products** across the Apple App Store and Google Play.
- Reduced build-to-tester delivery time from roughly **30 minutes to 10 minutes** through release automation.
- Contribute through architecture and feature design, peer code reviews, troubleshooting, technical documentation, and developer mentoring.
